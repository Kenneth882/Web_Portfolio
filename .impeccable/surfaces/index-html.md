OWN-WORLD: Pale cool paper, charcoal pixel typography, a red plastic Pokédex, green LCD, and red/blue/yellow/purple Poké Balls. Departure Mono throughout. Restrained background gives the colored objects authority.

STORY: See Kenneth's name, discover four project slots, click a ball to inspect a project, open the Pokédex for an introduction. Placeholder content is labeled honestly.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Tiny navigation and introductory copy at the upper corners. A large red Pokédex hangs from a charcoal lanyard at center. Four different balls flank it at varied heights. Oversized name marquee runs behind the bottom of the scene. Dragging the Pokédex releases a spring-driven swing. Project selection opens a readable panel with previous/next navigation.
